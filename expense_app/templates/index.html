⚡ How "Set Always" & Glob Rules Work

  • Zero friction: Once you check "Set Always" during expense upload, the app remembers the mapping forever.
  • Case-insensitive: "starbucks", "STARBUCKS", and "Starbucks" all match the same rule.
  • Exact match priority: Exact merchant matches always take priority over glob patterns.
  • Glob Pattern Options:
    • * — Matches any sequence of characters (e.g. PAYPAL * matches PAYPAL *SPOTIFY and PAYPAL *NETFLIX)
    • ? — Matches exactly one character (e.g. STORE ?? matches STORE 01)
    • [seq] — Matches any character in sequence (e.g. SHELL [0-9]*, [A-Z]*)
    • [!seq] — Matches any character NOT in sequence (e.g. STORE [!0-9]*)
  • Always editable: You can edit or delete rules anytime right here.
